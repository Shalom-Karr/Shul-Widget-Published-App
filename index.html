Android · Home-screen widget

Today's next minyan, at a glance.

Shul Widget puts your shul's davening times on your home screen. Pick your shul once. The widget keeps itself current.

Download latest APK Sideload-only. Supports Android 7.1.1 and up.

Features

  • Today's next minyan, refreshed automatically
  • Daily shiurim
  • Zmanim computed on-device — no network round-trip after first load
  • Auto-updates from this page on every release
  • Works for any Luach shul (CMH, Khal Yereim, Torah Life Kollel, …)

Install

  1. Download the APK from the latest release.
  2. Open it on your phone. Allow "Install from unknown sources" if asked.
  3. Long-press your home screen → Widgets → drag Shul Widget.
  4. Pick your shul. Done.
