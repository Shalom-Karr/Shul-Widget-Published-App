SK Luach · Android

Your shul's davening times, on your home screen.

A widget + full app for any Luach-powered shul. Pick your shul once. It keeps itself current — minyanim, shiurim, events, zmanim, and weather.

Sideload-only · Android 7.1.1+

What's inside

App

Six tabs

Today, Schedule, Zmanim, Weather, Settings. Admin tab appears automatically when you sign in.

Widget

Schedule + Weather

Two responsive widgets in three sizes. Large layout scrolls the full chronological day.

On-device

20 zmanim via KosherJava

No network round-trip after first load. Customize which appear in Settings.

Multi-tenant

One app, many shuls

CMH, Khal Yereim, Torah Life Kollel — and any future shul added to the registry.

Admin

CRUD on your phone

Sign in with your shul's Supabase credentials. Edit minyanim, shiurim, events, exceptions.

Weather

Open-Meteo, free

Current conditions + 5-day forecast. Standalone widget in the same visual language.

Install

  1. Tap Download latest APK above.
  2. Open the file on your phone. Allow "Install from unknown sources" if asked.
  3. Long-press your home screen → Widgets → drag Shul Widget out.
  4. Pick your shul. Done.
